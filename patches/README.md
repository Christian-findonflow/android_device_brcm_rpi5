# Patches to AOSP projects outside this device tree

Changes the NEO dash needs in AOSP modules that are not one of our forks.
Each sub-directory is named after the project path with `/` replaced by `_`,
and holds numbered `git diff` files against the 25Q4 release the tree is on.

Apply from the tree root after `repo sync`:

    device/brcm/rpi5/patches/apply.sh

The script is idempotent (`git apply --check` first, skips patches already in).

| Project | Patch | Why |
|---|---|---|
| packages/modules/Bluetooth | 0001-a2dp-sink-bounded-audio-write | The A2DP sink wrote PCM to its AudioTrack with a blocking write while holding the sink mutex. On the dash the track's output is the earbud A2DP link, whose start needs the stack's main thread, which was itself waiting on that mutex: three-way deadlock, stack frozen for minutes (2026-09-05, see docs/FOLLOW-UPS.md). The write is now non-blocking with a 20 ms grace period and drops what does not fit. Also gives the track an 8192-frame buffer (was 2052): the phone sends packets in bursts of 2-3 and the small buffer dropped one packet every few seconds, an audible tick. |
| packages/modules/Bluetooth | 0002-avrcp-scope-role-switch-close-to-peer | With a phone streaming into the sink and earbuds connecting as our sink, the earbuds' role switch closed the phone's AVRCP controller channel (AVRC_UpdateCcb closed every legacy channel, not just the earbuds'), the controller service dropped the phone as active device and the sink session ended ("Bluetooth audio disconnected"). Adds AVCT_GetPeerAddr and skips other peers' channels. |
| packages/modules/Bluetooth | 0003-bta-av-source-data-only-to-source-streams | With both profiles on, the phone's stream into our sink is "started" too and received the source data-ready event: the dash pushed the encoded earbud stream to the phone at full rate while the earbuds starved and dropped their link (radio log: 43 media packets/s to the phone, ~1/s to the earbuds). Guards bta_av_data_path and bta_av_dup_audio_buf on the local SEP direction. |
| packages/modules/Bluetooth | 0004-avdtp-only-transmit-streams-take-the-current-stream-slot | AVDTP lets one "current" stream transmit when several are streaming; it counted the phone's incoming stream, so the earbud stream never got the slot and every media packet was freed ("ignore AVDT_SCB_API_WRITE_REQ_EVT" 43x/s), the earbuds heard silence and sent SUSPEND after 3 s. Count only streams where our local SEP is the source. |
| packages/modules/Bluetooth | 0005-btif-av-retry-start-after-earbud-suspend | An earbud-initiated suspend is sticky in AOSP until the earbuds' AVRCP PLAY clears the flag; on the dash that key goes to the new AVRCP target profile and never reaches btif_av, and the audio HAL port stays STARTED so nothing re-issues START. In coexist mode, while the phone still streams into our sink, retry START at 2/5/10 s instead. |
| packages/modules/Bluetooth | 0006-avrcp-target-ignore-earbud-hand-back-pause | Earbuds that also belong to the phone (AirPods after Siri or a call) send PAUSE to the dash as the phone hands them back, even though the dash was not playing into them; the dash forwarded it to the phone and stopped the music the rider had just got back. Ignore a PAUSE for a stream that is not playing or started < 1.5 s ago. |
| packages/modules/Bluetooth | 0007-pbap-client-defer-download-no-photos-hf-20s-timeout | The phonebook download started with the other profiles and hogged the link (vCards with photos, 60 kB/s for 30 s): the hands-free SLC timed out (10 s, three retries) and the phone dropped the A2DP setup after its 5 s timer, so some boots came up with no audio and no calls. Defer PBAP by 15 s after connect (cancelled on ACL drop), stop requesting contact photos, and give the HF client SLC 20 s. |
| packages/modules/Bluetooth | 0008-hf-client-hci-voice-path-and-sco-bridge | Call audio through the dash. The hands-free client hardcoded offload=true in every eSCO parameter set, so the phone's voice link was routed to the controller's unwired PCM pins; it now follows `bluetooth.hfp.software_datapath.enabled` (data path = HCI). New `stack/btm/btm_sco_bridge.cc`: with `bluetooth.neo.sco_bridge.enabled`, every in-band SCO packet is forwarded verbatim to the other connected SCO link (phone <-> earbuds), re-packetised through a byte ring per link; same codec required on both links. Adds `btm_send_sco_packet_to()`. The Android audio framework cannot represent two SCO links, and the dash has no speaker or microphone of its own, so nothing else needs the voice. |
| packages/apps/Car/Settings | 0001-small-screen-sizes-and-wifi-password-dialog | The car Settings app assumes a tall car screen: 76 dp touch targets, 96 dp toolbar, big dialogs. On the 592x352 px panel this patch sets 48 dp touch targets, a 56 dp toolbar, 48 dp list rows, 32 dp icons and compact dialog margins, and replaces the Wi-Fi password dialog with a compact DialogFragment that fits. The toolbar margin (which car-ui-lib also uses as the back-arrow container width) is 44 dp so the arrow stays inside the panel and pressable. These edits existed uncommitted since 2026-08-30 and were captured as a patch on 2026-09-06. |
| frameworks/base | 0001-car-framework-sizes-for-800x480 | The car theme's framework sizes (dimens_car.xml: title/body text 32 sp and up, 96 dp list rows, 20 dp margins) and the car alert-dialog button bar are sized for a tall car screen; reduced for the 592x352 px panel. Uncommitted since 2026-08-30, captured 2026-09-06. |
